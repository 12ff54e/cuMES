Input boundary

The boundary moves to balance plasma and vacuum pressure. Coil fields are generated locally when you run.

Coil currents and field grid

Accepts MAKEGRID coils-dot and cumes-coils-v1 JSON. Currents are in amperes, in circuit order.

Field grid bounds and resolution

R and Z are in meters. The grid must cover the plasma throughout the solve; field periods must match the equilibrium.

Precision

Double uses two f32 words, not native IEEE fp64. Changing precision during a solve restarts it; your setup is retained.

Paired precision is recommended for free-boundary solves. Single precision is experimental and can stall on W7-X.
